Advanced Laparoscopic Surgery at Havya Women's Hospital

Laparoscopy, often called keyhole or minimally invasive surgery, is revolutionizing the way we diagnose and treat many gynecological and obstetric conditions. At Havya Women's Hospital, we bring precision, safety, and faster recovery to our patients through state-of-the-art laparoscopic procedures performed by experienced specialists.

What is Laparoscopy?

Laparoscopy is a surgical technique that uses small incisions and a high-definition camera (laparoscope) to view and treat internal organs. Unlike traditional surgery, this method is less painful, causes minimal scarring, and allows for quicker healing and shorter hospital stays.

Benefits of Laparoscopic Surgery

Laparoscopy offers significant advantages over conventional surgery:

  • Smaller incisions and minimal scarring
  • Reduced pain and bleeding
  • Shorter hospital stay and faster recovery
  • Lower risk of infections
  • Quicker return to normal activities
  • Better cosmetic outcomes
